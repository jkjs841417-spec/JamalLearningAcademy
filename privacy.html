Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jamal Learning Academy. This Privacy Policy explains how our learning platform may collect, use and protect information provided by students and visitors.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

  • Student name and mobile number.
  • Email address and basic profile information.
  • Course enrollment and learning progress.
  • Messages and support requests.
  • Technical information needed to operate the platform.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Provide access to courses and lessons.
  • Maintain learning progress and enrollment records.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Improve the platform and its services.
  • Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access.

4. Account Security

We aim to protect student information using appropriate security measures. Students should keep their passwords and verification details private and should not share account access with others.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell students' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the platform, provide support, or meet applicable legal requirements.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember certain preferences and improve the user experience. You can manage browser storage through your device settings.

7. Third-Party Services

Some platform services may rely on third-party providers for authentication, hosting, communication or video delivery. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

8. Student Rights

Students may contact the academy to ask about their personal information, request corrections, or raise concerns about how their information is handled, subject to applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

If a student is under the age of 18, a parent or legal guardian should review the applicable terms and provide any consent required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through our Contact Us page.

Important: This is a draft policy for the website. It must be reviewed and updated to reflect the academy's actual data collection, storage, service providers and legal obligations before the platform is launched.
